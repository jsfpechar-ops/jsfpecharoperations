# Production host UX corrections

Owner requirements captured 2026-10-12. Source reviewed: main `7b27d09402f9656b0c606c131793310925936526`. PR #378 is merged; it delivered the navigation plan, not these corrections. This document takes precedence over conflicting optional-platform, active-toggle, address-parser or onboarding instructions in that plan. No application change has been implemented or tested by the orchestrator in this review.

## What the owner wants

1. **Less is more across the host app.** Keep the familiar section header bars, make navigation clear, reduce clutter and mandatory steps, and use reliable defaults. Remove backend explanations from normal user flows. Reveal help when the user needs it; retain actionable errors and required legal acceptance.
2. **Keep one maintained editor per setting.** Properties use Overview / Bookings & calendars / Settings. Guest links, business/legal details, fees and locks must reuse their canonical fields and handlers. Overview cards navigate and show summaries; they do not contain duplicate settings forms.
3. **Upgrade Add property.** Replace the basic, long form and numbered stepper with a polished short form: property name, optional city, required operator; structured address can be added now or later. Calendar, reporting, guest links and optional locks are contextual follow-up actions. Creating a property should not require a tour through every feature.
4. **Restore separate address fields everywhere.** Remove the single-line input, parsing, promises to split the address, nested “address sent to police” wrapper and parser-specific warnings. District, municipality, municipality part, street, house number, orientation number and postcode stay available with their existing validation. Save what the host typed; do not guess legal address components.
5. **Simplify police reporting.** Remove “Details sent to the police (filled for you)” and explanations of automatic derivation. Show the actual fields with short labels. Keep matching the police register, credentials, test connection, errors and real filing safeguards. Credential guidance becomes contextual ? help, not several paragraphs before the fields.
6. **Remove the data-box send/open shortcut.** Do not advertise a working send integration. Keep working copy/download of prepared request/report documents and manual filing records. The exact Czech “Poslat datovou schránkou” button is not present at the reviewed source; the confirmed external shortcut is in the fee detail template. Deployment/source drift must be reported rather than inventing a match.
7. **Guest links stay.** Default editable message: “Welcome! Please complete the guest registration form before your stay. If you have any questions, please contact your host. Thank you!” Czech: “Vítejte! Před pobytem prosím vyplňte registrační formulář hostů. Pokud máte jakékoli dotazy, obraťte se na svého hostitele. Děkujeme!” Do not promise completion time, refer to an absent “link below”, overwrite custom messages, or bulk rewrite existing records.
8. **A property always has an operator.** Remove `(none)` and duplicate operator controls. Select the sole available operator automatically; with several operators require a deliberate selection. With none, offer business creation and preserve the property draft. Enforce ownership and presence on the server, including onboarding and forged requests.
9. **Operator is the default guest data controller and contact.** This is a legal entity, not the physical property. Remove the main-form checkbox and explanatory block. Keep existing legitimate alternative-controller records and one advanced override when needed. Defaults must not silently replace established legal identity/contact.
10. **Remove the active/sync toggle.** New properties and ordinary saves of non-archived properties are active regardless of submitted `active` values. Archive remains the way to stop using a property; archived records stay inactive. No bulk reactivation migration. This does not enable automatic police sending: new properties retain Manual mode and existing reporting modes stay unchanged.
11. **Calendar platform AND URL are required.** Owner explicitly confirmed this after clarification. Platform is a visible first field, never under More options: Booking.com, Airbnb, Vrbo, Agoda, e-chalupy or Other. Other requires a readable platform name. The URL must be valid and non-empty; no unnamed connection, logo-only label or named draft without URL. Derive a readable connection name from the platform unless the host supplies an optional custom name. Preserve old feeds and show readable fallbacks without a destructive migration.
12. **Fix lock navigation.** Connect/check/error actions stay in the relevant lock setup section. Return to the property's expanded lock section after success; do not jump to the page top. Reduce initial lock instructions to three essentials with the complete provider instructions in Guidance or contextual ? help. Preserve terms, admin sharing, ownership, existing assignment and PIN lifecycle.
13. **Fix fitting and horizontal sliding.** Normal stays fit the available content lane. Dates, property, source, guest count, status, deadline and actions remain readable and reachable. Adapt to sidebar width and zoom; compact labeled cards replace a dense table before it becomes cramped. Do not hide overflow, cut off dates/actions, make text tiny, or remove supported actions to force a fit.
14. **Audit the other host pages for the same failures.** Dashboard, Properties, Stays/detail, reports/register, business/invoice settings, invoices, fees, account settings, guest-link and automation hubs, lock setup and first-login paths. Use the same spacing, field/error/action patterns; do not restyle public/guest pages or change legal export content incidentally.
15. **Staging first; production only after approval.** Execute focused correction briefs, collect actual browser evidence, then stage the exact candidate for owner review. Main currently triggers production CI deployment; do not merge to main just to obtain a preview.

## Final navigation and optional-feature requirements

- Put Change PIN / Generate new PIN and Change link / Generate new link near the current guest URL/PIN, at the top of Guest links. Preserve the actual distinction: editing the readable name is not rotating the permanent link; regenerating the link invalidates old access and may rotate PIN. Keep the existing confirmations; routine helper prose is contextual ? help.
- Move Archive property / Restore out of Guest links into Settings → Property information, separated from ordinary saves. Preserve the existing archive/restore handlers and their consequences. The owner's wording is understood as rejecting its current placement under Guest links.
- Digital door lock uses a polished switch with concise label **Automatic door codes**, and an explicit persisted **Enabled / Off** status. No sentence-length checkbox label “Create a door code for each stay at this property”. Use the same switch/status presentation for existing genuine optional-feature booleans elsewhere; legal acceptance and data fields are not feature switches. Property activity remains always-on, with no switch.
- Enabling a switch shows an unsaved draft until saved; do not call it enabled/connected when an attempted save failed. Existing test-mode warning, lock selection, eligibility, check-in/out times and actual failure status remain visible. The two “How door codes work” links become one ?/Guide destination.
- Interactive optional setup: create property → immediately offer Connect calendar or Add stay manually → guest links ready to use → reporting setup when needed. Calendar is early, not at the bottom of a long settings document. After connection, collapse the onboarding invitation into a compact real status; the connection workflow is reached by a clear Add calendar action rather than permanently filling the overview with inputs. No forced lock/fee/invoice steps or misleading five-step progress.
- Implement the approved property **Overview / Bookings & calendars / Settings** navigation for staging. Bookings/calendars is visible in the main local bar. Keep shortcut cards, one canonical editor, stable old hashes, drafts and errors; do not create a second standalone Settings editor.
- Whole-host ownership/navigation from the already-approved maintainability plan still applies to business/legal details, guest links, automation, fees, invoices, account, Dashboard and Stays. 0094 replaces the stale property extraction/tab/calendar contracts; the remaining scoped canonical-host tasks are rebased against these corrections before release. Do not revive contradictory old copy, More options or active/parser controls.

## Latest clarification: visible controls, contextual help

The owner subsequently rejected arrow/More options sections that hide useful configuration. This clarification supersedes the earlier disclosure suggestions above and in the illustrative mockup:

No More options or Suggested portal message. Remove that suggested-message block and its duplicate copy buttons; keep Preview as a normal action. Generic guest-form message is a different feature and stays.
- Important property settings show ordinary section headers and visible fields inside the selected section. Keep the familiar header bar appearance; do not use a chevron to conceal essential values. Tabs/section navigation choose the relevant editor; no duplicated hidden desktop/mobile forms.
- Routine field descriptions move to a consistent ? beside the label, with hover, keyboard-focus and tap access. Examples include both guest-link day-window explanations, PIN help, address formats, calendar URL instructions and settings helper text. Long guidance goes to the existing Guide. Do not replace every paragraph with another always-visible notice.
- Errors, current state, required markers, actual entered values, destructive-action confirmation and acceptance controls remain visible. “Hide explanations” must not hide important status or confirmations. Tooltip-only `title` is insufficient for touch/keyboard users.
- House book keeps the table the owner likes. Remove the legal-duty accordion and legal lede; a small ? links to /guide#housebook. Existing legal text lives in Guidance, not the working register.
- Create's address card is directly visible and compact; the mockup's “Add now” chevron is illustrative and must be omitted. Calendar custom name is an ordinary optional field, not another More options disclosure. Full lock/credential help uses the same ?/Guide pattern.
- One reusable host help component handles this across all named host families. No hover-only hints, lost accessible descriptions, duplicate IDs, page-wide blanket hiding, or buried PIN. Execute 0093 after the focused corrections to complete the consistent host-wide pass.

## Proposed visual direction

[Add-property mockup](production-host-ux-designs/property-create.png) uses fictional values. It is a visual reference, not application evidence or a new approval of implementation. Use existing app typography/icons/tokens and light mode; no new font, third-party script or dependency.

Warm ivory canvas, restrained terracotta accents, clear white cards, balanced spacing. Keep one short primary column and an optional narrow follow-up panel on wide screens. On small screens the form becomes one column and follow-up links move below it. No numbered stepper, active switch or controller checkbox. Use ordinary visible field cards rather than chevrons. Address fields are normal inputs, not a parser. The mockup's address values are fictional; never encode them as defaults. The owner explicitly dislikes “filled for you” and backend explanations: omit them even if an older design includes them.
