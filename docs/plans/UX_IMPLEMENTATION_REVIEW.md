# UbyHost — review of the UX/UI audit implementation (and stay-fee steps 1–7b)

**Reviewed:** `main` @ `ecfc24a` (2026-09-26). The audit landed at `4a9786e`, and this review covers everything from there to HEAD: 160 UX commits (PRs #116, #117, #118), the owner-decision commit `20c04b6`, and stay-fee steps 1–7b.
**How:** seven parallel reviewers, one per surface (A guest · B auth · C host ×2 · D public · E e-mail) plus one for the stay fee. Each diff was checked against its `UX_AUDIT.md` item. Pages and mails were rendered through the real app in EN and CS, HTML was checked with a spec parser, and suspected bugs were reproduced with TestClient scripts or traced line by line in the JS (there is no browser in the sandbox). Nothing in the repo was modified.
**Tests:** **1790 pass on Python 3.12**, the version CI and the Dockerfile use. On Python 3.10, 22 fail, but only because of a test-fixture issue (see nit N-1); the product code isn't at fault.

## Verdict

**The work is faithful and careful.** 151 of 162 backlog items have their own commit, and most of the rest were done inside other commits or in the owner-decision commit. EN/CS parity holds everywhere, no test assertion was weakened, and there's no dark mode, new dependency, XSS or open redirect. No guest page points to support@. The one blocker still open is UX-9, the public "Try UbyHost" CTA. The problems are concentrated in a few places:

- the new "Move to a new phone" route (security);
- the stay-fee total (money);
- the guest wizard's history and signature pad;
- a handful of host-app state and copy edges.

Reviewer scope: UX-1, 2, 10–20, 49–61, 113–122 (commit map in `/tmp/rev/A_commits.txt`), plus owner-decision commits `20c04b6` (UX-117) and `489790e` (language default).

### Summary

Overall this is a faithful, careful implementation. Every UX item in scope has a commit. The copy is close to the audit wording in both EN and CS. I found no tests that were weakened: every removed assertion was replaced by one that pins the new copy. There is no XSS, no new dependency, and no support@ address on guest pages.

The weak spot is `signature.js`, in the wizard's history handling and the signature pad's sizing. The in-page Back button pushes history entries, so after any in-page Back the phone's back gesture moves *forward*. That forward move skips step validation. The canvas is also sized, and the kept signature repainted, while the step is hidden. The second problem existed before this work, but UX-57's new copy now tells the guest a signature is shown when the box is empty.

Counts: **bug-critical 0 · bug-major 2 · bug-minor 6 · deviation 5 · nit 12**.
