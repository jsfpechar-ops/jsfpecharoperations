## 1. Objective

Fix the two defects the Fable diff review of 1072 found:

1. **Blocking: an alert can vanish.** In `_host_alerts.html`, with `open_alerts_more == 1` and every kept non-critical row dropped by `alerts.present_many`, `rest_count == 1` makes `rest_inline` true, so neither a card nor the "N alerts need you" summary renders. The 13th could be a filing alert at warning level (`submission_stuck`, raised as `"warning"` at `App/app/reporting.py:1768-1770`).
2. **Server-Timing on public pages.** `_signed_in_page` (`App/app/main.py:367-375`) excludes only guest links (`/l`) and `/cleaning/shared/`. A signed-in host who opens a public token page or a legal page still gets the header. Exclude them too.
