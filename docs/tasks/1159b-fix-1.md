## 1. Objective

The review of executed 1159b found two gaps in `go/internal/calendar/filing_watchdog_run.go`:

1. `watchdogAddress` flags as unreadable (error log, stay left unmailed) contact addresses that Python's `mail.normalise_email` mails. The realistic case is a Czech company name: `Apartmány s.r.o. <jan@example.test>`.
2. Four `slog.Error` calls drop the `err`.

Widen the address grammar, but only where Go then gives exactly Python's address. Add `err` to the four logs. Add the missing digest case: at-risk stays with no unknown ones. Nothing is wired; production is unchanged.

## 2. Context

Rules 1, 3 (fixture addresses: `example.test`) and 4 (stdlib only: `unicode/utf8`). The patch (block 1) changes three 1159b files:

- **`filing_watchdog_run.go`**:
  - **Address characters.** `watchdogAddrChar` is ASCII atext without the backtick, plus U+00C0-U+017F without `×`, `÷` and `İ` (U+0130; Python lower-cases it to two characters).
  - **Local part.** It may have leading, trailing or double dots (Python keeps them as written).
  - **Domain labels.** They may hold `_` and those letters, with an optional final dot.
  - **Display name.** `watchdogDisplayName` is any run of characters other than `"()<>[]:;@\,`, backslash and controls (tab allowed), plus quoted strings. So non-ASCII, dots, and commas inside quotes are all read.
  - **Length.** The 254 limit is now counted in characters (`utf8.RuneCountInString` on the lower-cased address), as Python counts it, not in bytes.
  - **Logs.** `"err", err` is added to `push_deadline_failed`, `deadline_at_risk mail failed`, `unknown-risk query failed` and `deadline digest failed`.
- **`App/scripts/filing_watchdog_run_golden.py`**:
  - 12 new `CONTACTS`.
  - The builder `digest_at_risk_only`, with 2 stays and 0 unknown.
- **`filing_watchdog_run_test.go`**:
  - The pins change to 19 forms read like Python and 20 flagged. Before they were 7 and 20. `J. Doe <…>`, `hé@…` and `"Host, One" <…>` now read like Python.
