## 1. Objective

The review of PR #944 found that all four golden instants are midday CEST in September, so a Go bug that read the clock in UTC, used a fixed offset, or flipped a `<`/`<=` would still pass. Add 7 seed stays and 11 instants that pin the Prague clock (CET, CEST, both sides of the March and October 2026 DST changes, UTC and Prague dates differing) and both sides of each comparison; and let `UBYHOST_SUBMIT_SWEEP_MINUTES` take digit-group underscores (`1_0`) as Python's `int()` does. Nothing is wired; production is unchanged.
