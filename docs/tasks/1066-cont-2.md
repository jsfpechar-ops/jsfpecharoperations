## 1. Objective

CI on PR #860 (browser shard 2) fails `tests/test_push_settings_browser.py::test_the_alerts_row_fits_wide_layouts` (all four params, 1280 and 2000, collapsed or not) at line 152 with `assert 6 == 0`: six elements in `#settings-group-phone` have scrollWidth > clientWidth.
