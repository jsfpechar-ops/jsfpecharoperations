## 1. Position in one paragraph

UbyHost has two AI providers: **Mistral AI SAS (France)** and **Anthropic, PBC (USA)**. `UBYHOST_AI_PROVIDER` picks one for all AI features at a time (default `anthropic`; production target `mistral`). Both are listed as subprocessors, both are conditional (only when a host uses Havel, or a guest chats with Leon on a property whose host switched Leon on). The texts say "our AI providers" and name both, so they stay true whichever one is configured and need no change on a rollback.

## 2. Assumptions about Mistral (checked 2026-11-15 against Mistral's own pages; re-check before live text changes)

- **M1 Zero data retention (ZDR) exists but is not on by default.** Mistral docs: ZDR "controls whether supported API inputs and outputs are retained after Mistral returns a response". It covers stateless calls including `/v1/chat/completions` (what UbyHost uses), embeddings, OCR, moderation. It does **not** cover Agents, Conversations, Libraries, Batch, `/v1/files`, Chat (Le Chat) or Studio traces. Eligibility: the docs say "paid plans"; the help center article says "pay-as-you-go" only. Mistral reviews each request and "may approve or deny" it, so it is **per organisation, on request, at Mistral's discretion**. Once approved it shows in the Admin panel under Privacy. Sources: docs.mistral.ai/admin/monitor-comply/zero-data-retention; help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347612-can-i-activate-zero-data-retention-zdr.
- **M2 Without ZDR, abuse monitoring applies.** Mistral's DPA section 2.3 covers "abuse monitoring on our APIs" and excludes it when "zero data retention has been activated". The DPA sets no retention period. Secondary sources quoting Mistral's developer terms say API inputs and outputs are kept 30 rolling days for abuse monitoring. **Not confirmed from a primary page I could open; counsel or Mistral support must confirm the number.** Mistral may act as an independent controller for that abuse monitoring (DPA, as summarised by third parties; confirm).
- **M3 UbyHost does not have ZDR.** Until the owner gets it approved and sees it in the Admin panel, the texts must not say "zero retention" or "not stored".
- **M4 Training.** ZDR and the training opt-out are separate. The opt-out is the Admin panel > Privacy > "Anonymous improvement data" toggle (covers Studio and API). The default for paid API use is not stated on the pages I could open. Owner must switch the toggle off and note the date here: <owner: date>.
- **M5 Location.** `api.mistral.ai` (what our code calls, `go/internal/anthropic/mistral.go`) is the **global** endpoint: "Mistral does not commit to a specific inference location". `api.eu.mistral.ai` (EU and EFTA data centres) and `api.us.mistral.ai` are regional, at 1.1 times the price. Regional guarantees exclude Agents, Batch and Files, and control-plane data (account, keys, billing, usage analytics) may be handled elsewhere. An older help article says data is hosted in the EU by default; the newer regional-inference page does not support that for the global endpoint, so **do not claim EU-only processing** unless the code is switched to the EU endpoint (owner decision, section 6).
- **M6 Transfers.** The DPA (section 8) allows transfers to adequate countries and otherwise relies on "adequate safeguards" or the SCCs. It does not mention the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Mistral is established in France, so sending data to it is not a third-country transfer in itself; a transfer arises when Mistral or its subprocessors process outside the EEA (possible on the global endpoint). Anthropic: SCCs, as in havel-ai.md A2 and A3.
- **M7 Subprocessors and audit.** Mistral keeps a subprocessor list on its website and Trust Center, gives "reasonable notice" of changes, and customers may object within 10 days (DPA 7.1 and 7.2). Breach notice "without undue delay" (no hour limit). One on-site audit a year with 90 days notice. The DPA is at legal.mistral.ai/terms/data-processing-addendum; it must be accepted for our account (check the console: <owner: date and who>).

## 6. What the owner does (in order)

1. console.mistral.ai > Admin > Billing: move the organisation to a paid / pay-as-you-go plan (ZDR is not offered on the free tier).
2. Admin > Privacy: switch **"Anonymous improvement data"** off. Write the date in M4.
3. Open help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347612-can-i-activate-zero-data-retention-zdr and send the ZDR request (Help Center form or support). Give a legitimate reason: guest-facing chat for accommodation providers, personal data of guests possible, GDPR processor duties, only stateless `/v1/chat/completions`. Ask in the same message: (a) is ZDR available on our plan, (b) the exact retention without ZDR, (c) which data centres serve `api.mistral.ai`, (d) the current subprocessor list.
4. Wait for approval. Approval shows in Admin > Privacy; a rejection arrives by e-mail. If approved, tell us: the texts then say "zero data retention is activated for our account" and drop the 30-day sentence for Mistral. If rejected, the texts stay as written.
5. Accept the DPA for the account (legal.mistral.ai/terms/data-processing-addendum) and record date and person in `docs/vendors/README.md`.
6. Decide the endpoint: global (today, cheapest, no location promise) or `api.eu.mistral.ai` (EU/EFTA data centres, +10%, then the texts can say EU processing, except control-plane data). Switching is a one-line URL change in `go/internal/anthropic/mistral.go` and `App/app/ai.py` (brief 1792-code).
7. Subscribe to Mistral's subprocessor updates (Trust Center) and book a re-check of these assumptions each quarter.
